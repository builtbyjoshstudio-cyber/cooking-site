Scratch Chocolate Chip Cookie Dough

Chocolate chip cookie dough, start to finish in two and a half minutes. White sugar, brown sugar and a lot of butter creamed in the stand mixer — then vanilla and eggs, the dry mix a little at a time, and mini semi-sweet chips folded in last. No talking, just the mixer and the music.

This is the first bake on the channel. Josh cooks, Jordan bakes — and this dough becomes chocolate chip cookie cups in the next video.

Ingredients listed make approximately three (3) dozen cookies. The video shows the recipe doubled (approximately six (6) dozen cookies).

Ingredients

Exact measurements — makes approximately 3 dozen cookies (the video doubles this batch for ~6 dozen).

Cookie Dough

  • 1 cup unsalted butter (softened to room temperature)
  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 2 eggs
  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • ½ teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon sea salt
  • 2 cups chocolate chips (but really, measure with your heart)

Instructions

  1. Combine the white sugar, brown sugar, and butter together in a stand mixer.

  2. Add the eggs and vanilla, and then beat on medium-high speed.

  3. In a separate bowl, mix flour, baking soda, baking powder, and salt.

  4. Slowly add the dry ingredients to the dough in the stand mixer.

  5. Stir in the chocolate chips.
