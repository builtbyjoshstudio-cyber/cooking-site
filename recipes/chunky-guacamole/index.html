Chunky Knife-Cut Guacamole

Avocados, cherry tomatoes, shallot, serrano, fresh lime, salt. Chunky rather than smooth — folded by hand at the end instead of mashed, with no cups and no measuring spoons anywhere in it.

It genuinely only takes about ten minutes, which is why the full-process session at normal speed runs ten minutes and not an hour.

What's on the Board

Base & Board

  • Avocados
  • Cherry tomatoes (quartered)
  • Shallot (diced fine)
  • Serrano pepper

Acid & Seasoning

  • Fresh lime juice
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Red pepper flakes

Pan Order & Method

  1. Score and scoop the avocados: Halve the avocados, pop the pits, score the flesh into clean cubes inside the skin, and scoop into a mixing bowl. Do not mash.

  2. Knife-cut the rest of the board: Quarter the cherry tomatoes, finely dice the shallot, and mince the serrano.

  3. Season with acid and heat: Add fresh lime juice, salt, black pepper, and red pepper flakes.

  4. Fold gently—don't mash: Fold everything together with a light hand so the avocado cubes stay distinct and chunky rather than breaking down into a smooth puree. Taste and adjust salt, lime, and heat.

What to Look & Taste For

  • Avocados soak up more salt and lime than you expect—taste a full bite with tomato and serrano together before calling it done.
