Weeknight Ground Beef Bulgogi

Bulgogi without the thin-sliced ribeye and without the restaurant bill. Ground beef, a marinade of serranos, garlic, soy, honey and ginger — and one thing you're not expecting in it: fresh grapefruit juice. The beef browns and comes out so the vegetables fry in its fat, then everything goes back in and the sauce reduces down to a finish of cold butter.

Served over rice measured the way Josh's dad taught him: water to the first knuckle, no cup involved.

What's on the Board

Ballpark shopping counts so you know what to grab at the store—dry spices, salt, and oil stay by feel.

Base

  • Jasmine rice

Protein

  • 1 lb ground beef

Marinade

  • ~1 cup low-sodium soy sauce
  • ~½ cup honey
  • Juice of 1 whole grapefruit
  • 2 serrano peppers
  • 6 cloves garlic
  • ~1 tsp ground ginger (or equal parts fresh ginger to garlic)
  • Black pepper (by feel)

Pan Veg

  • 1 big shallot (thin-sliced)
  • 7 mini sweet peppers (stripped — not traditional, still going in)
  • 8 cloves garlic

Finish

  • Cold butter (a couple pats, by feel)

Pan Order & Method

  1. Build the marinade: Combine soy sauce, honey, garlic, ginger, serranos, black pepper, and fresh-squeezed grapefruit juice.

  2. Start the rice & prep the veg: Rinse jasmine rice until the water runs clear (about seven cycles), then measure the water using the knuckle method. Thin-slice the shallots and strip the mini sweet peppers while the rice cooks and the skillet heats up.

  3. Brown the beef, then strain it: Brown the ground beef hard in a hot stainless skillet, then pull and strain it so you decide how much rendered fat goes back in.

  4. Fry the veg in the beef fat: Drop a controlled amount of that rendered beef fat back into the skillet and fry the shallots and sweet peppers until softened and colored.

  5. Reduce the sauce, then kill the heat for the butter: Return the beef to the pan, pour in the grapefruit marinade, and let it reduce. Turn the burner off first, then stir in cold butter until the sauce turns glossy and coats every bite. Serve over hot jasmine rice.

What to Look & Taste For

  • Balance salty soy, sweet honey, serrano heat, and bright grapefruit acidity before it hits the pan.
  • Never add the cold butter while the burner is still cranked—killing the heat first emulsifies it into a glossy glaze instead of breaking into grease.
