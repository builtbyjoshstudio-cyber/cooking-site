One-Pan Ground Beef Taco Bowls

Skip the store-bought seasoning packet. Brown the beef in your own spice blend, pull it out so the peppers and shallots fry directly in the rendered beef fat, and fold it all back together.

Updated November 26, 2026
Pan / ToolHot skillet
Cook StyleBy feel · No measuring cups
CategoryOne-Pan Dinners

What's on the Board

Protein & Spice Blend

  • Ground beef
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Garlic powder
  • Onion powder
  • Ground cumin
  • Paprika
  • Cayenne

The Board

  • Jalapeño
  • Garlic
  • Mini sweet peppers
  • Shallots
  • Cherry tomatoes

Bowl Build

  • Tortilla chips (two ways)
  • Chunky guacamole

Pan Order & Method

  1. Prep all produce on one board: Dice/slice the jalapeño, garlic, mini sweet peppers, shallots, and fresh cherry tomatoes (keep the cherry tomatoes aside for topping).

  2. Brown beef in its own spice blend: Brown the ground beef in a hot skillet with salt, black pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, cumin, paprika, and cayenne.

  3. Pull the beef, keep the fat: Pull the browned beef out of the skillet and leave the seasoned beef fat behind.

  4. Fry the veg in the beef fat: Cook the mini sweet peppers, shallots, jalapeño, and garlic directly in that rendered beef fat until softened and fragrant.

  5. Fold back together & build bowls: Return the beef (fat included) to the skillet, toss everything together, and build bowls over tortilla chips topped with fresh cherry tomatoes and chunky guacamole.

What to Look & Taste For

  • Taste the beef before pulling it—layering cumin, paprika, garlic/onion powder, and cayenne directly into the browning meat blooms the spices in the fat.
