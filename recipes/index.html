Recipes — By-Feel Savory Playbooks & Scratch Baking

Josh's savory recipes are written as by-feel playbooks; Jordan's scratch baking cards use exact measurements.

Josh • By Feel Stainless skillet & rice cooker

Weeknight Ground Beef Bulgogi

Bulgogi without the thin-sliced ribeye and without the restaurant bill. Ground beef, a marinade of serranos, garlic, soy, honey and ginger — and one thing you're not expecting in it: fresh grapefruit juice. The beef browns and comes out so the vegetables fry in its fat, then everything goes back in and the sauce reduces down to a finish of cold butter.

One-Skillet Spicy Sausage Hash

Sausage, potatoes, carrots, sweet peppers, a jalapeño, onion, and garlic — all in one skillet, and the only thing you have to get right is the order things go in the pan. The sausage browns first so everything after it cooks in that fat. A splash of chicken stock goes in, the lid goes on, and it cooks down until the broth turns into a glaze.

Josh • By Feel Bare stainless skillet & rice cooker

One-Pan Steak & Pepper Rice Bowls

Steak and pepper rice bowls, the whole cook. Rice goes on first. Then jalapeños, a shallot and a pile of mini sweet peppers come off one board. The steak gets sliced into strips and seasoned right in the bowl — avocado oil, salt, cayenne, black pepper, ground cumin and fresh lime juice — then it hits a hot stainless pan, the peppers go in on top, and everything simmers down in its own juices. A second hit of seasoning in the pan, then it all goes over rice.

One-Pan Ground Beef Taco Bowls

Build-your-own beef taco bowls, start to finish. Jalapeño, garlic, mini sweet peppers, shallots and cherry tomatoes all come off one board. The beef browns in its own seasoning blend — salt, black pepper, garlic and onion powder, cumin, paprika, cayenne — then comes out so the vegetables can cook in the beef fat, and everything, fat included, goes back in.

Scratch Chocolate Chip Cookie Dough

Chocolate chip cookie dough, start to finish in two and a half minutes. White sugar, brown sugar and a lot of butter creamed in the stand mixer — then vanilla and eggs, the dry mix a little at a time, and mini semi-sweet chips folded in last. No talking, just the mixer and the music.
