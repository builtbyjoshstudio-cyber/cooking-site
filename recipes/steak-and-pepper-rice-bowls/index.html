One-Pan Steak & Pepper Rice Bowls

Rice goes on first, all the produce comes off one board while it cooks, and steak strips get seasoned twice—once in the bowl, once in the pan—before simmering down with sweet peppers in their own juices. No measuring cups from start to bowl.

Updated November 26, 2026
Pan / ToolBare stainless skillet & rice cooker
Cook StyleBy feel · No measuring cups
CategoryOne-Pan Dinners

What's on the Board

Base

  • Rice

Protein & Bowl Seasoning

  • Steak (sliced into strips)
  • Avocado oil
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Cayenne
  • Ground cumin
  • Fresh lime juice

The Board

  • Jalapeños
  • Shallot
  • Mini sweet peppers

Pan Order & Method

  1. Start the rice first: Get your rice going with the finger method before touching the cutting board.

  2. Prep all produce on one board: Slice the jalapeños, shallot, and a pile of mini sweet peppers while the rice cooks.

  3. Season the steak in the bowl: Slice steak into strips and toss directly in a bowl with avocado oil, salt, cayenne, black pepper, ground cumin, and fresh lime juice.

  4. Sear hot, pile peppers on top, simmer down: Hit a hot bare stainless skillet with the seasoned steak strips, pile the peppers, jalapeños, and shallot right on top, and let everything simmer down together in its own juices.

  5. Second hit of seasoning & serve: Taste and hit the pan with a second round of seasoning to dial in heat, salt, and lime, then pile straight over hot rice.

What to Look & Taste For

  • Coating the steak in avocado oil and lime right in the bowl helps the cumin and cayenne cling evenly before hitting the stainless steel.
  • Pull when the peppers are tender-crisp and the pan juices have reduced enough to coat the rice.
